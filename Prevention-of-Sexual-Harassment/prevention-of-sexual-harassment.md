# Prevention of Sexual Harassment.

## What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Commenting on another person’s personality or appearance.
- Asking unwarranted personal questions.
- Eve teasing.
- Whistling, touching, stroking someone’s hair.
- Unwelcome repeated requests for dates or outings.
- Gender-based insults.

Such behaviour creates a hostile, or uncomfortable work environment and is strictly prohibited.

---

## What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?

### If I Face Harassment:

- I'll clearly tell the person that their behaviour is not good and ask them to stop.
- If the behaviour continues, I will report the matter to the higher authority.
- Document everything, including date, time, place, witnesses, and details of the incident.
- I'll submit a written complaint to the ICC.
- If the situation involves assault or serious misconduct, I will file a police complaint.


### If I Witness Harassment:

- I will encourage the affected person to file a complaint.
- If necessary, I can also file a complaint on their behalf.
