# Question 1 : What is Deep Work?

Deep work is the ability to focus on a task without distractions. It involves concentration that improves skills and productivity.

---

# Question 2 : According to the author, how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

1. Schedule deep work sessions at a fixed time daily, for at least 4 hours.
2. Create a distraction-free environment.
3. Plan the work that you are going to do before starting.
4. Train the brain to focus for long hours.

---

# Question 3 : How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?

1. Schedule a particular time to do deep work at the same time every day.
2. Create a distraction-free environment.
3. Maintain a to-do list and complete tasks daily.
4. Get disconnected from all social media.

---

# Question 4 : What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

1. Causes distraction.
2. Reduces the ability to concentrate.
3. Consumes a lot of time unknowingly.
4. Reduces productivity.
